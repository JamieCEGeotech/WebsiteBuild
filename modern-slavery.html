Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking Statement 2025-2026

Introduction
This statement sets out CE Geotech’s actions to understand all potential modern slavery risks related to its business and to put in place steps that are aimed at ensuring that there is no slavery or human trafficking in its own business and its supply chains. This statement relates to actions and activities during the financial year 2025-2026.

Our Commitment
CE Geotech is committed to preventing slavery and human trafficking in its corporate activities, and to ensuring that its supply chains are free from slavery and human trafficking.

Organisational Structure and Supply Chains
CE Geotech provides geochemical consultancy and laboratory services. The company operates in the UK and works with suppliers both domestically and internationally.

Policies
CE Geotech has policies in place to prevent modern slavery and human trafficking, including:

  • Anti-slavery and human trafficking policy
  • Whistleblowing policy
  • Code of conduct
These policies are reviewed annually and communicated to all staff.

Due Diligence
The company undertakes due diligence when considering taking on new suppliers, and regularly reviews its existing suppliers. The due diligence process includes:

  • Assessing risks in the supply chain
  • Requiring suppliers to confirm compliance with anti-slavery policies
  • Monitoring supplier performance

Risk Assessment and Management
CE Geotech evaluates the risk of modern slavery in its operations and supply chains and takes steps to mitigate any identified risks.

Training
All employees receive training on modern slavery and human trafficking, helping them to identify and report concerns.

Board Approval
This statement has been approved by the Board of Directors and will be reviewed and updated annually.

Contact
For further information, please contact CE Geotech’s compliance team.

## Why Ground Investigation Matters

Every successful build starts with understanding the ground beneath it. Poor or incomplete site data can lead to:
- **Unexpected costs** during construction
- **Delays** in planning approval
- **Structural risks** that compromise safety

Our investigations reduce uncertainty, identify hazards, and provide the foundation for safe, efficient design.

![caption](Files/010.webp)
- Review of historical maps, environmental data, and site history
- Identification of potential contamination or geotechnical risks
- Essential for planning applications and lender requirements

### **Phase 2 Intrusive Site Investigation**
- Boreholes, trial pits, and in-situ testing (SPT, CBR, plate load)
- Laboratory testing for soil strength, contamination, and classification
- Detailed interpretative reports with foundation recommendations

### **CBR Testing**
- On-site or laboratory California Bearing Ratio testing
- Supports pavement and highway design
- Fast turnaround for time-sensitive projects

### **Slope Stability & Foundation Advice**
- Analysis of embankments, cuttings, and retaining structures
- Recommendations for strip, raft, or piled foundations
- Advice tailored to your soil conditions and design requirements
